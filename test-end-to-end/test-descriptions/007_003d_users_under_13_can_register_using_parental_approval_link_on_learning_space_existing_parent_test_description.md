# When registration is enabled: Users under 13 can register using parental approval link,On learning space,where parent is an existing user

## Description:

When registration is enabled: Users under 13 can register using parental approval link

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Login as admin.
2. Click on "Settings" icon.
3. Click on "Site" button.
4. Click on "Edit" button.
5. Enter Company terms in "Terms and policies" text field.
6. Click on "Registration allowed" switch.
7. Click on "Save" button 
8. Clear app storage.
9. Launch the app and click "New User".
10. Choose "Learning space".
11. Enter birthdate for a child aged under 13 (e.g., 10 years).
12. Click "Next".
13. Ensure new terms screen does not appear.
14. Enter child’s full name.
15. Select gender.
16. Enter parent's email address.
17. Click "Other options".
18. Choose to sign up using username and password.
19. Enter child’s username and password.
20. Click "SIGN-UP".
21. Verify "Wait for Parent" consent screen appears.
22. Click "OK".
23. Ensure return to home screen and verify "New User" button is visible.
24. Clear app storage.
25. Launch the app and click "New User".
26. Enter birthdate for parent (e.g., 30 years).
27. Click "Next".
28. Accept terms and conditions.
29. Enter parent's full name.
30. Select gender.
31. Click "Next".
32. Enter parent’s username and password.
33. Click "SIGN-UP".
34. Verify redirection to "Courses" page, indicating successful login.
35. Clear app storage.
36. Open the parental approval link received via email.
37. On the account selection screen, click "Parent User".
38. Verify "Consent management" screen is displayed.
39. Confirm consent details and relationship (default: Father).
40. Verify terms and policies are shown.
41. Click "I consent".
42. Confirm navigation to child personal detail screen.
43. Ensure child profile "Child User" is visible.
