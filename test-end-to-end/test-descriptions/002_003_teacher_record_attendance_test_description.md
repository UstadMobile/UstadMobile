# Teacher can record attendance.

## Description:

Teacher can record attendance. Teacher can edit attendance after recordings

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Using http api endpoint created a class with students and teachers already enrolled and setup.
2. Launch Ustad app
3. Click on "Existing User" button
4. Click on "Enter link manually" button
5. Enter the site link
6. Login as teacher
7. Click on "Courses".
8. Click on course name.
9. Click on "Attendance".
10. Click on "Record attendance".
11. Click on "Next" button.
12. Click on "Mark all present" button.
13. Click on "Late" button next to a student.
14. Click on "Save" button.
15. Click on "Courses" button.
16. Click on course name.
17. Click on "Attendance".
18. Scroll down and click on the attendance already added.
19. Click on "Mark all present" button.
20. Click on the "Save" button.
21. Verify that the changes made by teacher is reflected on students attendance.
