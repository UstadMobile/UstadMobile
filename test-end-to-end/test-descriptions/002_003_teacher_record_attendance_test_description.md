# Teacher can record attendance.

## Description:

Teacher can record attendance. Teacher can edit attendance after recordings

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Launch Ustad app 
2. Click on "Existing User" button  
3. Click on "Enter link manually" button  
4. Enter the site link  
5. Login as admin.
6. Click on "Courses."
7. Click on the "+ Course" button.
8. Click on the "Add a new course" button.
9. Enter a course name.
10. Click on the "Description" field.
11. New text block appears.
12. Enter the description.
13. Click on the "Done" button.
14. Click "Save" button to save the course.
15. Click on the "Courses"
16. Click on the course name.
17. Click on the "Members" tab.
18. Click on the "Add a teacher" button
19. Click on "Add a new person" button
20. Enter the teacher's first name, last name and Gender.
21. Click on the "Save" button and save the person details.
22. Click on the "Save" button to save the enrollment details.
23. Click on the teacher name.
24. Click on the "View profile" button.
25. Click on the "Create account" button.
26. Enter the username.
27. Enter the password.
28. Click on "People".
29. Click on "+ Person" button.
30. Click on "Bulk import" button.
31. Click on "Select file" button.
32. Select the csv file.
33. Click on "Import"
34. Verify the import success message.
35. Clear the app storage.
36. Login to the app using the teacher username and password.
37. Click on "Courses".
38. Click on course name.
39. Click on "Attendance".
40. Click on "Record attendance".
41. Click on "Next" button.
42. Click on "Mark all present" button.
43. Click on "Late" button next to a student.
44. Click on "Save" button.
45. Click on "Courses" button.
46. Click on course name.
47. Click on "Attendance".
48. Scroll down and click on the attendance already added.
49. Click on "Mark all present" button. 
50. Click on the "Save" button. 
51. Verify that the changes made by teacher is reflected on students attendance.
