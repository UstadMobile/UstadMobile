# Admin or teacher can add a content block and upload new content via file upload

## Description:

Admin or teacher can add a content block and upload new content via file upload

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Launch Ustad app 
2. Click on "Existing User" button  
3. Click on "Enter link manually" button  
4. Enter the site link  
5. Login as admin.
6. Click on "Courses."
7. Click on the "+ Course" button. 
8. Click on the "Add a new course" button. 
9. Enter a course name. 
10. Click on the "Description" field. 
11. Enter the description. 
12. Click on the "Done" button. 
13. Click the "Save" button to save the course. 
14. Click on the "Courses"
15. Click on the course name. 
16. Click on the "Members" tab. 
17. Click on the "Add a teacher" button 
18. Click on "Add a new person" button 
19. Enter the teacher's first name, last name and Gender.
20. Click on the "Save" button and save the person details.
21. Click on the "Save" button to save the enrollment details.
22. Click on the teacher name.
23. Click on the "View profile" button. 
24. Click on the "Create account" button. 
25. Enter the username. 
26. Enter the password. 
27. Click on "Courses"
28. Click on Course name. 
29. Click on the "Edit" button. 
30. Click on the "Add block" button. 
31. Click on the "Content" button. 
32. Click on "Import from file".
33. Select the H5p content.
34. Click on "Done" to save the content block.
35. Click on "Save" to save the course. 
36. Clear app storage. 
37. Login to the app using the teacher username and password.
38. Click on "Courses"
39. Click on Course name. 
40. Click on the "Edit" button. 
41. Click on the "Add block" button. 
42. Click on the "Content" button.
43. Click on "Import from file".
44. Select the epub content. 
45. Click on "Done" to save the content block. 
46. Click on "Save" to save the course. 
47. Verify the course contains both H5p and Epub content.
