# When registration is enabled: Users under 13 can register using parental approval link ,On Personal space,where parent is a new user

## Description:

When registration is enabled: Users under 13 can register using parental approval link

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Login as admin.
2. Click on "Settings" icon.
3. Click on "Site" button.
4. Click on "Edit" button.
5. Enter Company terms in "Terms and policies" text field.
6. Click on "Registration allowed" switch.
7. Click on "Save" button
8. Clear app storage.
9. Launch app and click "New User".
10. Choose "Personal" space.
11. Enter birthdate for a child aged under 13 (e.g., 10 years old).
12. Click "Next".
13. Verify no new terms prompt is shown.
14. Enter child's full name.
15. Select gender.
16. Enter parent’s email.
17. Click "Next".
18. Verify "Wait for Parent" screen is shown.
19. Click "OK" to return to new/existing user screen.
20. Clear app storage.
21. Open the parental approval link received via email.
22. Launch app through the link.
23. Click "New User".
24. Enter birthdate for a parent (e.g., 30 years old).
25. Click "Next".
26. Accept terms and conditions.
27. Enter parent's full name.
28. Select gender.
29. Verify parent tick mark is shown.
30. Choose to sign up using username and password.
31. Enter username and password.
32. Click "Signup".
33. Verify successful login.
34. Verify child profile is listed.
35. Click on the child profile.
36. Verify child details (name, gender, DOB).
37. Click "Save" and then "Done".
38. Verify "Select account to start with" popup is shown.
39. Verify both child and parent profiles are listed.
40. Click on the child's name.
41. Verify access to courses and library features.
