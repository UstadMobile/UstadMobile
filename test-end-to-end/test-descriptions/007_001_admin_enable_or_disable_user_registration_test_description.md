# Admin can enable/disable registration for users

## Description:

Admin can enable/disable registration for users

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Launch Ustad app
2. Click on "Existing User" button
3. Click on "Enter link manually" button 
4. Enter the link in "Site link" field 
5. Click on "Next" button 
6. Enter admin credentials and login 
7. Click on "Settings" icon. 
8. Click on "Site" button. 
9. Click on "Edit" button. 
10. Enter Company terms in "Terms and policies" text field. 
11. Click on "Registration allowed" switch. 
12. Click on "Save" button 
13. Clear app storage. 
14. Open the app 
15. Click on the "New User"
16. Click on the "Join Learning Space"
17. Click on "Enter link manually"
18. Enter the site link in "Site link" field. 
19. Click on the "Next" button 
20. Click on the "Next" button on select Date Of Birth page
21. Verify user gets an error "This field is required."
22. Select the date of birth, age 13+ 
23. Click on the "Next" button 
24. Verify the entered "Terms and policies" visible to user.
25. Click on the "Accept" button
26. If platform supports passkeys, click on "Other options"
27. Verify user gets an error "This field is required." just below Full name and gender fields.
28. Enter Full name.
29. Select Gender
30. If platform supports passkeys, click on "Other options".
31. Click on "Signup" button
32. Verify user gets an error "This field is required." just below Username and password fields.
33. Enter username and password
34. Click on "Signup" button
35. User logged into the app
36. User directed to courses section
37. Verify user able to view Courses, Messages and People tabs
38. Clear app storage. 
39. Open the app 
40. Click on "Existing user" button 
41. Click on "Learning Space" button 
42. Click on "Enter link manually" button 
43. Enter the link in "Site link" field 
44. Click on "Next" button 
45. Enter admin credentials and login 
46. Click on "Settings" icon. 
47. Click on "Site" button. 
48. Click on "Edit" button. 
49. Click on "Registration allowed" switch to disable registration.
50. Click on "Save" button 
51. Clear app storage. 
52. Click on the "New User"
53. Click on the "Join Learning Space"
54. Click on "Enter link manually"
55. Enter the site link in "Site link" field. 
56. Click on the "Next" button 
57. Verify user able to see an error saying this learning space does not allow new user registration.
