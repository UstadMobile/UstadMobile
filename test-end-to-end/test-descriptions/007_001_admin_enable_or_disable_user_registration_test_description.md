# Admin can enable/disable registration for users

## Description:

Admin can enable/disable registration for users

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Launch the Ustad app.
2. Click “Existing User”.
3. Click “Enter link manually”.
4. Enter the Site link.
5. Click “Next”.
6. Enter admin username and password, then login.
7. Click on the Settings icon.
8. Click on “Site” tab.
9. Click “Edit”.
10. Enable the Registration allowed switch.
11. Click “Save” button.
12. Verify warning appears: “If self-registration is enabled, you must set terms and policies in at least one language for users to accept when they register.”
13. Turn off the Registration allowed switch.
14. Click “Save” button.
15. Click the Edit icon for Terms and policies.
16. Enter new text like “New Terms” in the Terms and policies field.
17. Enable the Registration allowed switch again.
18. Click “Save”.
19. Verify confirmation prompt with “Yes” appears.
20. Clear app storage. 
21. Open the app 
22. Click on the "New User"
23. Click on the "Join Learning Space"
24. Click on "Enter link manually"
25. Enter the site link in "Site link" field. 
26. Click on the "Next" button 
27. Click on the "Next" button on select Date Of Birth page
28. Verify user gets an error "This field is required."
29. Select the date of birth, age 13+ 
30. Click on the "Next" button 
31. Verify the entered "Terms and policies" visible to user.
32. Click on the "Accept" button
33. If platform supports passkeys, click on "Other options"
34. Verify user gets an error "This field is required." just below Full name and gender fields.
35. Enter Full name.
36. Select Gender
37. If platform supports passkeys, click on "Other options".
38. Click on "Signup" button
39. Verify user gets an error "This field is required." just below Username and password fields.
40. Enter username and password
41. Click on "Signup" button
42. User logged into the app
43. User directed to courses section
44. Verify user able to view Courses, Messages and People tabs
45. Clear app storage. 
46. Open the app 
47. Click on "Existing user" button 
48. Click on "Learning Space" button 
49. Click on "Enter link manually" button 
50. Enter the link in "Site link" field 
51. Click on "Next" button 
52. Enter admin credentials and login 
53. Click on "Settings" icon. 
54. Click on "Site" button. 
55. Click on "Edit" button. 
56. Click on "Registration allowed" switch to disable registration.
57. Click on "Save" button 
58. Clear app storage. 
59. Click on the "New User"
60. Click on the "Join Learning Space"
61. Click on "Enter link manually"
62. Enter the site link in "Site link" field. 
63. Click on the "Next" button 
64. Verify user able to see an error saying this learning space does not allow new user registration.
