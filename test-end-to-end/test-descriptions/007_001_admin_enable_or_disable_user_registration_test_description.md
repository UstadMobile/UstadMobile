# Admin can enable/disable registration for users

## Description:

Admin can enable/disable registration for users

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Launch Ustad app
2. Click on "Existing User" button
3. Click on "Enter link manually" button
4. Enter the site link 
5. Open the app 
6. Click on "Existing user" button 
7. Click on "Learning Space" button 
8. Click on "Enter link manually" button 
9. Enter the link in "Site link" field 
10. Click on "Next" button 
11. Enter admin credentials and login 
12. Click on "Settings" icon. 
13. Click on "Site" button. 
14. Click on "Edit" button. 
15. Enter Company terms in "Terms and policies" text field. 
16. Click on "Registration allowed" switch. 
17. Click on "Save" button 
18. Clear app storage. 
19. Open the app 
20. Click on the "New User"
21. Click on the "Join Learning Space"
22. Click on "Enter link manually"
23. Enter the site link in "Site link" field. 
24. Click on the "Next" button 
25. Select the date of birth, age 13+ 
26. Click on the "Next" button 
27. Verify the entered "Terms and policies" visible to user.
28. Clear app storage. 
29. Open the app 
30. Click on "Existing user" button 
31. Click on "Learning Space" button 
32. Click on "Enter link manually" button 
33. Enter the link in "Site link" field 
34. Click on "Next" button 
35. Enter admin credentials and login 
36. Click on "Settings" icon. 
37. Click on "Site" button. 
38. Click on "Edit" button. 
39. Click on "Registration allowed" switch to disable registration.
40. Click on "Save" button 
41. Clear app storage. 
42. Click on the "New User"
43. Click on the "Join Learning Space"
44. Click on "Enter link manually"
45. Enter the site link in "Site link" field. 
46. Click on the "Next" button 
47. Verify user able to see an error saying this learning space does not allow new user registration.
