# Group users can add assignment and private comments

## Description:

Teacher and students can submit and view private comments. Students who are in the same group see the same set of comments. Comments are private within the group.

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Launch Ustad app
2. Click on "Existing User" button
3. Click on "Enter link manually" button
4. Enter the site link 
5. Login as admin.
6. Click on "Courses."
7. Click on the "+ Course" button.
8. Click on the "Add a new course" button.
9. Enter a course name.
10. Click on the "Description" field.
11. New text block appears.
12. Enter the description.
13. Click on the "Done" button.
14. Click "Save" button to save the course.
15. Click on the "Courses"
16. Click on the course name.
17. Click on the "Members" tab.
18. Click on the "Add a teacher" button
19. Click on "Add a new person" button
20. Enter the teacher's first name, last name and Gender.
21. Click on the "Save" button and save the person details.
22. Click on the "Save" button to save the enrollment details.
23. Click on the teacher name.
24. Click on the "View profile" button.
25. Click on the "Create account" button.
26. Enter the username.
27. Enter the password.
28. Click on the "Save button"
29. Click on "People".
30. Click on "+ Person" button.
31. Click on "Bulk import" button.
32. Click on "Select file" button.
33. Select the csv file.
34. Click on "Import"
35. Verify the import success message.
36. Clear the app storage
37. Login to the app using the teacher username and password.
38. Click on "Courses" tab.
39. Click on the course name.
40. Click on "Edit" button.
41. Click on "Add block" button
42. Click on "Assignment".
43. Enter the assignment title in the "Title" field.
44. Enter the assignment description in the "Description" field.
45. Click on "Group Submission" switch
46. Click on "Groups" field.
47. Click on "Add new groups".
48. Enter a group title in "Title field"
49. Enter 2 in "Number of groups" field.
50. Assign first 3 students to Group 1.
51. Assign last 3 students to Group 2.
52. Click on "Save" button to save group details.
53. Set the submission policy to "Must submit all at once".
54. Click on the "Done" button.
55. Click on the "Save" button.
56. Click on the assignment.
57. Click on the "Submission" tab 
58. Click on group 1. 
59. Enter a comment on "Add a private comment" field. 
60. Click on "Send" icon. 
61. Clear the app storage 
62. Login to the app using the student username and password in group 1. 
63. Click on "Courses" tab. 
64. Click on the course name. 
65. Click on the assignment added by teacher. 
66. Enter a comment on "Add a private comment" field. 
67. Click on "Send" icon. 
68. Verify that student's and teacher's class comment is visible for the students in group 1. 
69. Login to the app using the student username and password in group 2. 
70. Click on "Courses" tab. 
71. Click on the course name. 
72. Click on the assignment added by teacher. 
73. Verify that private comments are not visible for the students in group 2.
