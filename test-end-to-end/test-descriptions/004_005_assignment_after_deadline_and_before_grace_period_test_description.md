# Assignment is submitted after the deadline but before the grace period, the submission should be accepted.

## Description:

If assignment is submitted after the deadline but before the grace period, the submission should be accepted. The specified late penalty should be applied to the mark given by the teacher.


## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Launch Ustad app  
2. Click on "Existing User" button  
3. Click on "Enter link manually" button  
4. Enter the site link  
5. Login as admin.
6. Click on "Courses."
7. Click on the "+ Course" button.
8. Click on the "Add a new course" button.
9. Enter a course name.
10. Click on the "Description" field.
11. New text block appears.
12. Enter the description.
13. Click on the "Done" button.
14. Click "Save" button to save the course.
15. Click on the "Courses"
16. Click on the course name.
17. Click on the "Members" tab.
18. Click on the "Add a teacher" button
19. Click on "Add a new person" button
20. Enter the teacher's first name, last name and Gender.
21. Click on the "Save" button and save the person details.
22. Click on the "Save" button to save the enrollment details.
23. Click on the teacher name.
24. Click on the "View profile" button.
25. Click on the "Create account" button.
26. Enter the username.
27. Enter the password.
28. Click on the "Save button"
29. Click on "People".
30. Click on "+ Person" button.
31. Click on "Bulk import" button.
32. Click on "Select file" button.
33. Select the csv file.
34. Click on "Import"
35. Verify the import success message.
36. Clear the app storage
37. Login to the app using the teacher username and password.
38. Click on "Courses" tab.
39. Click on the course name.
40. Click on "Edit" button.
41. Click on "Add block" button
42. Click on "Assignment".
43. Enter the assignment title in the "Title" field.
44. Enter the assignment description in the "Description" field.
45. Enter yesterday's date and time in "Deadline" field.
46. Enter tomorrow's date and time in "End of grace period" field.
47. Enter a number in "Late submission penalty" field. 
48. Set the submission policy to "Must submit all at once". 
49. Click on the "Done" button. 
50. Click on the "Save" button. 
51. Clear the app storage
52. Login to the app using the student username and password.
53. Click on "Courses" tab. 
54. Click on the course name. 
55. Click on the assignment added by teacher. 
56. Enter the assignment text in the provided text field. 
57. Click on "Add file" button. 
58. Attach the required file. 
59. Click on the "Submit" button. 
60. Verify the submit status changed to "Submitted". 
61. Clear the app storage 
62. Login to the app using the teacher username and password.
63. Click on "Courses" tab. 
64. Click on the course name. 
65. Click on the assignment. 
66. Click on the "Submissions" tab. 
67. Click on the specific student's name.
68. Review the submitted text 
69. Open the submitted file. 
70. Click on "Mark" field. 
71. Enter the mark 
72. Click on the "Submit grade" button.
73. Verify that the mark is reduced according to the late penalty percentage.
74. Clear the app storage. 
75. Login to the app using the student username and password.
76. Click on "Courses" tab. 
77. Click on the course name. 
78. Click on the assignment. 
79. The student should see their graded assignment and the marks. 
80. Verify that the mark is reduced according to the late penalty percentage
