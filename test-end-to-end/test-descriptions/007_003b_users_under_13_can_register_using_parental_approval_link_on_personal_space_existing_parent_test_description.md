# When registration is enabled: Users under 13 can register using parental approval link,On Personal space,where parent is an existing user

## Description:

When registration is enabled: Users under 13 can register using parental approval link

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Login as admin.
2. Click on "Settings" icon.
3. Click on "Site" button.
4. Click on "Edit" button.
5. Enter Company terms in "Terms and policies" text field.
6. Click on "Registration allowed" switch.
7. Click on "Save" button
8. Clear app storage.
9. Launch app and click "New User".
10. Choose "Personal" space.
11. Enter birthdate for a child aged under 13 (e.g., 10 years old).
12. Click "Next".
13. Verify no new terms prompt is shown.
14. Enter child's full name.
15. Select gender.
16. Enter parent’s email.
17. Click "Done".
18. Verify "Wait for Parent to consent" screen is shown.
19. Clear app storage.
20. Launch app and click "New User".
21. Enter birthdate for the parent (e.g., 30 years old).
22. Click "Next".
23. Accept terms and conditions.
24. Enter parent's full name.
25. Select gender.
26. Click "Next".
27. Enter username and password.
28. Click "Sign-up".
29. Verify user lands on the home screen with Courses visible.
30. Clear app storage.
31. Open the parental approval link received via email.
32. Launch app through the link.
33. Click on the existing "Parent User" account.
34. Verify list of child profiles is shown.
35. Click on the "Child User" profile.
36. Verify child's details (name, gender, DOB).
37. Click "Save".
38. Verify landing on home screen with Courses visible.
