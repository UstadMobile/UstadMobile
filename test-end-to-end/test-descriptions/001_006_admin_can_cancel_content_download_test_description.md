# Admin can cancel content download and download it again

## Description:

Content download can be canceled. After cancellation, the download stops and it can be
downloaded again. (Android and Desktop only).

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Go to File-Downloads and make sure content file is there in downloads for test
2. Launch Ustad app 
3. Click on "Existing User" button  
4. Click on "Enter link manually" button  
5. Enter the site link  
6. Login as admin.
7. Click on "Library."
8. Click on the "+ Content" button.
9. Click on the "From file" button.
10. Select the content file.
11. Click the "Save" button.
12. Verify that the content is visible in the library.
13. Click on the content .
14. Ensure the content opens without issues.
15. Click "Back" to return to the library.
16. Clear app storage and restart the app.
17. Login as admin.
18. Click on "Library"
19. Click on the content to select it.
20. Click the "Download" button.
21. Click on the "Cancel" button
22. The download button will be visible again.
23. Click on the "Download" button.
24. Click on the "Open" button. 
25. Verify the content appears as expected.
