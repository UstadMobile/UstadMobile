# Assignment is set to be marked by peers and submitted by groups

## Description:

If assignment is set to be marked by peers and submitted by groups, where each assignment is marked by two peers, students can see assignment submissions for those that their group has been assigned to mark. Students can mark each others work according to the peer review allocation (as per group membership). Students will see the marks submitted by their peers (other groups).

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Using http api endpoint created a class with students and teachers already enrolled and setup.
2. Launch ustad app
3. Click on "Existing User" button
4. Click on "Enter link manually" button
5. Enter the site link and click Next
6. Login to the app using the teacher username and password.
7. Click on "Courses" tab.
8. Click on the course name.
9. Click on "Edit" button.
10. Click on "Add block" button
11. Click on "Assignment".
12. Enter the assignment title in the "Title" field.
13. Enter the assignment description in the "Description" field.
14. Click on "Group Submission" switch
15. Click on "Groups" field.
16. Click on "Add new groups".
17. Enter a group title in "Title field"
18. Enter 2 in "Number of groups" field.
19. Assign first 3 students to Group 1.
20. Assign last 3 students to Group 2.
21. Click on "Save" button to save group details.
22. Set the submission policy to "Must submit all at once".
23. Click on "Marked by" drop down.
24. Select "Peers" option.
25. Enter 1 in "Reviews per user or group" field.
26. Click on "Assign Reviewers" button.
27. Click on "Assign random reviewers" button. 
28. Click on the "Done" button to save reviewers.
29. Click on the "Done" button to save assignment
30. Click on the "Save" button to save course.
31. Clear the app storage 
32. Login to the app using the student username and password in group 1. 
33. Click on "Courses" tab. 
34. Click on the course name. 
35. Click on the assignment added by teacher. 
36. Enter the assignment text in the provided text field. 
37. Click on the "Submit" button. 
38. Verify the submit status changed to "Submitted". 
39. Clear the app storage 
40. Login to the app using the student username and password in group 2. 
41. Click on "Courses" tab. 
42. Click on the course name. 
43. Click on the assignment added by teacher. 
44. Click on the "Peers to review" tab.
45. Click on "Group 1".
46. Review the Group 1 submitted text. 
47. Click on "Mark" field. 
48. Enter the mark 
49. Click on the "Submit grade" button.
50. Clear the app storage 
51. Login to the app using the student username and password in group 1. 
52. Click on "Courses" tab. 
53. Click on the course name. 
54. Click on the assignment. 
55. The student should see their graded assignment and the marks given by the peer.
