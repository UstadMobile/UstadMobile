# When registration is enabled: Users under 13 can register using parental approval link ,On learning space,where parent is a new user

## Description:

When registration is enabled: Users under 13 can register using parental approval link

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Login as admin.
2. Click on "Settings" icon.
3. Click on "Site" button.
4. Click on "Edit" button.
5. Enter Company terms in "Terms and policies" text field.
6. Click on "Registration allowed" switch.
7. Click on "Save" button
8. Clear app storage.
9. Launch the app and click "New User".
10. Choose "Learning space".
11. Enter birthdate for a child aged under 13 (e.g., 10 years).
12. Click "Next".
13. Ensure no new terms are shown.
14. Enter child’s full name.
15. Select gender.
16. Enter parent's email address.
17. Click "Other options".
18. Choose to sign up using username and password.
19. Enter username and password.
20. Click "SIGN-UP".
21. Verify "Wait for Parent" consent screen appears.
22. Click "OK".
23. Back on the welcome screen, verify "New User" button is visible.
24. Clear app storage.
25. Open the parental consent link received via email.
26. Launch the app from the email link.
27. Click on "New User".
28. Enter birthdate for the parent (e.g., 30 years).
29. Click "Next".
30. Accept terms and conditions.
31. Enter parent’s full name.
32. Select gender.
33. Click "Other options".
34. Enter username and password.
35. Click "SIGN-UP".
36. Verify "Consent management" screen appears.
37. Verify consent details and default relationship (e.g., Father).
38. Verify terms and policies are displayed.
39. Click on "I consent".
40. Verify navigation to child’s personal detail screen.
41. Confirm child name and details are visible.
