# Add new course, add members to that course, add a course banner and admin user can copy the course

## Description:

Admin can create a new course, add a new teacher,add a new student, add a course banner pic and admin user can copy a course.

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Log in as admin user with credentials 'admin' and 'testpass'.
2. Click on the 'Courses' tab.
3. Click on the 'Course' button.
4. Click on 'Add a new course'.
5. Click 'Save' without entering a course name.
6. Verify validation error appears for course title.
7. Enter 'Test Course Block' in the course title field.
8. Click the 'Add Photo' icon.
9. Upload 'courseBannerPic.jpg' file.
10. Click the 'Save' button.
11. Click on 'Members' button.
12. Click on 'Add a teacher'.
13. Add a new person with role 'Teacher 1', gender 'Female'.
14. Click on 'Add a student'.
15. Add a new person with role 'Student 1', gender 'Male'.
16. Verify 'Members' button is visible.
17. Click on 'Student 1'.
18. Click on 'View profile'.
19. Create user account with username 'stud1' and password 'tests1'.
20. Navigate back to course page.
21. Click on 'Teacher 1'.
22. Click on 'View profile'.
23. Create user account with username 'teach1' and password 'testt1'.
24. Click on 'Permissions'.
25. Click on 'Edit'.
26. Click on 'Add new courses' permission.
27. Click on 'Save'.
28. Click on 'Library'.
29. Click on 'Courses'.
30. Click on 'Test Course Block'.
31. Click on 'Edit'.
32. Click on 'Add block'.
33. Select 'Module'.
34. Enter 'Term 1' as the module title.
35. Click on 'Done'.
36. Verify 'Term 1' is visible.
37. Click on 'Save'.
38. Click on 'Edit'.
39. Click on 'Add block'.
40. Select 'Assignment'.
41. Enter 'Assignment 1' in the title.
42. Set 'cbDeadlineDate' to one year in the past.
43. Click on 'Done'.
44. Verify 'Assignment 1' is visible.
45. Set 'clazz_start_time' to two years in the past.
46. Set 'clazz_end_time' to one year in the past.
47. Add a discussion board titled 'Discussion 1'.
48. Click on 'Save'.
49. Click on 'Library'.
50. Click on 'Courses'.
51. Click on 'All'.
52. Click on 'Test Course Block'.
53. Click on 'Discussion 1'.
54. Click on 'Post'.
55. Enter 'Post 1' as title and 'Discussion post' as content.
56. Click on 'Post'.
57. Verify 'Post 1' is visible.
58. Click on 'Courses'.
59. Click on 'Test Course Block'.
60. Click on 'Copy'.
61. Verify 'Copy course' is visible in the app bar.
62. Verify course name is 'Copy of Test Course Block'.
63. Verify 'Term 1' exists.
64. Verify 'Assignment 1' exists.
65. Scroll to bottom and click on 'Assignment 1'.
66. Set 'cbDeadlineDate' to tomorrow.
67. Click on '#caSubmissionPolicy'.
68. Select 'Can make multiple submissions'.
69. Click on 'Done'.
70. Clear and update course title to 'Copy of Test Course Block'.
71. Set 'clazz_start_time' to yesterday.
72. Clear 'clazz_end_time'.
73. Click on 'Add block'.
74. Select 'Text'.
75. Enter 'Text 1' in the title.
76. Click on 'Done'.
77. Verify 'Text 1' is visible.
78. Click on 'Save'.
79. Verify 'Save' button disappears.
80. Verify 'Term 1', 'Assignment 1', and 'Text 1' exist.
81. Click on 'Discussion 1'.
82. Verify 'Post 1' is not visible.
83. Click on 'Courses'.
84. Verify 'Copy of Test Course Block' exists.
85. Log in as teacher using credentials 'teach1' and 'testt1'.
86. Click on 'All'.
87. Verify 'Test Course Block' exists.
88. Click on 'Test Course Block'.
89. Verify 'Copy' button is visible.
90. Log in as student using credentials 'stud1' and 'tests1'.
91. Click on 'All'.
92. Verify 'Test Course Block' exists.
93. Click on 'Test Course Block'.
94. Verify 'Copy' button is not visible.
