# Add new course, add members to that course and add a course banner

## Description:

Admin can create a new course, add a new teacher,add a new student and add a course banner pic.

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Login as admin.
2. Click on "Courses."
3. Click on the "+ Course" button.
4. Click on the "Add a new course" button.
5. Leave the course title blank.
6. Click the "Save" button.
7. Verify that an error message saying "This field is required" appears below the course title field.
8. Enter a course title in the "Name" field.
9. Click on the "Save" button.
10. Click on the "Description" field.
11. New text block appears.
12. Enter the description.
13. Click on the "Done" button.
14. Click "Save" button to save the course.
15. Click on "Edit" button
16. Click on the "Add banner Picture" icon.
17. Click on "Select picture from files."
18. Select the picture from the file.
19. Click "Save" button.
20. Verify the Course banner picture is visible .
21. Click on the "Courses"
22. Click on the course name.
23. Click on the "Members" tab.
24. Click on the "Add a teacher" button
25. Click on "Add a new person" button
26. Enter the teacher's first name, last name and Gender.
27. Click on the "Save" button and save the person details.
28. Click on the "Save" button to save the enrollment details.
29. Click on the teacher name.
30. Click on the "View profile" button.
31. Click on the "Create account" button.
32. Enter the username.
33. Enter the password.
34. Click on the "Save button"
35. Click on the "Courses"
36. Click on the course name.
37. Click on the "Members" tab.
38. Click on the "Add a student" button
39. Click on "Add a new person" button
40. Enter the student's first name, last name and Gender.
41. Click on the "Save" button and save the person details.
42. Click on the "Save" button to save the enrollment details.
43. Click on the student name.
44. Click on the "View profile" button.
45. Click on the "Create account" button.
46. Enter the username.
47. Enter the password.
48. Click on the "Save button"
49. Clear the app storage.
50. Login to the app using the teacher username and password.
51. Verify the teacher able to see the course
52. Clear the app storage.
53. Login to the app using the student username and password.
54. Verify the student able to see the course
