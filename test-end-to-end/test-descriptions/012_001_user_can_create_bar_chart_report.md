# Users can generate a bar chart report.

## Description:

Admin or teacher can add or customize reports so that they can track progress.

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Login as admin.
2. Click on "Courses."
3. Click on the "+ Course" button. 
4. Click on the "Add a new course" button. 
5. Enter a course name. 
6. Click on the "Description" field. 
7. Enter the description. 
8. Click on the "Done" button. 
9. Click the "Save" button to save the course. 
10. Click on the "Courses"
11. Click on the course name. 
12. Click on the "Members" tab. 
13. Click on the "Add a teacher" button 
14. Click on "Add a new person" button 
15. Enter the teacher's first name, last name and Gender.
16. Click on the "Save" button and save the person details.
17. Click on the "Save" button to save the enrollment details.
18. Click on the teacher name.
19. Click on the "View profile" button. 
20. Click on the "Create account" button. 
21. Enter the username. 
22. Enter the password. 
23. Click on Save button
24. Click on "People".
25. Click on "+ Person" button.
26. Click on "Bulk import" button.
27. Click on "Select file" button.
28. Select the csv file.
29. Click on "Import"
30. Verify the import success message.
31. Click on "Courses"
32. Click on Course name. 
33. Click on the "Edit" button. 
34. Click on the "Add block" button. 
35. Click on the "Content" button. 
36. Click on "Import from file".
37. Select the H5p content.
38. Click on "Done" to save the content block.
39. Click on "Save" to save the course. 
40. Clear app storage. 
41. Login to the app using the student 1 username and password.
42. Click on "Courses"
43. Click on Course name. 
44. Click on the content
45. Go through the content
46. Clear app storage.
47. Login to the app using the student 2 username and password.
48. Click on "Courses"
49. Click on Course name.
50. Click on the content
51. Go through the content
52. Clear app storage.
53. Login to the app using the teacher username and password.
54. Click on "Reports"
55. Click on "Add report"
56. Enter the "Report title"
57. Select X-axis field as "Day"
58. Enter a series title
59. Select Y-axis field as "Content usage- total duration"
60. Select Sub-group by field as "Gender"
61. Select the chart type as "Bar chart"
62. Select the Time range as "This week"
63. Click on "Add filter" button
64. On Add filter page select field as "Person Age"
65. Select condition field as ">="
66. Enter value "10"
67. Click on "Done" button
68. On Edit report page click on "Done"
69. Verify the user able to see the bar chart as per user selection
