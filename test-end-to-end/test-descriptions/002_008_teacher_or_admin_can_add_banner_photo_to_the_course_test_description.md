# Teacher/admin can add banner photo to the course

## Description:

Teacher/admin can add banner photo to the course that is displayed in the course list screen and course detail screen.

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Launch Ustad app  
2. Click on "Existing User" button  
3. Click on "Enter link manually" button  
4. Enter the site link  
5. Login as admin.
6. Click on "Courses."
7. Click on the "+ Course" button.
8. Click on the "Add a new course" button.
9. Enter a course name.
10. Click on the "Description" field.
11. New text block appears.
12. Enter the description.
13. Click on the "Done" button. 
14. Click on the "Add banner Picture" icon. 
15. Click on "Select picture from files."
16. Select the picture from the file.
17. Click "Save" button.
18. Verify the Course banner picture is visible .
