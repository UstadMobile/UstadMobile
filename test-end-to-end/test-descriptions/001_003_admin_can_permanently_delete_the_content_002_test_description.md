# Admin can permanently delete content

## Description:

Admin can permanently delete content in the Library. Once deleted, the content cannot be restored.

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Launch Ustad app
2. Click on "Existing User" button  
3. Click on "Enter link manually" button  
4. Enter the site link  
5. Log in as admin.
6. Click on "Library."
7. Click on the "+ Content" button.
8. Click on the "From file" button.
9. Select the content file.
10. Click the "Save" button.
11. Verify that the content is visible in the library.
12. Long press on the content that needs to be deleted.
13. Confirm that the delete icon appears in the title bar.
14. Click on the "Delete" icon.
15. Verify that the content is not visible in the library.
16. Clear app storage and restart the app.
17. Login as admin.
18. Click on the "Delete" icon.
19. Verify that the content is not visible in the library.
20. Go to "Settings.
21. Click on "Deleted Items.
22. Click on the "Permanently Delete" button next to the content.
23. Confirm the permanent deletion.
24. Clear app storage and restart the app.
25. Login as admin.
26. Go to the "Library.
27. Verify that the content is no longer visible in the library.
