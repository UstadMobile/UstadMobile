# Admin can move content

## Description:

An attempts list on content page shows the completion status or score of user attempts.

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Login as admin.
2. Click on "Library."
3. Click on the "+ Content" button.
4. Click on the "From file" button.
5. Select the content file.
6. Click the "Save" button.
7. Verify that the content is visible in the library.
8. Click on the content.
9. Wait for the importing and uploading to complete.
10. Click the "Open" button.
11. Verify the content appears as expected.
12. Close the content.
13. Click on "Courses."
14. Click on the "+ Course" button. 
15. Click on the "Add a new course" button. 
16. Enter a course name. 
17. Click on the "Description" field. 
18. New text block appears. 
19. Enter the description. 
20. Click on the "Done" button. 
21. Click "Save" button to save the course. 
22. Click on the "Courses"
23. Click on the course name. 
24. Click on the "Members" tab. 
25. Click on the "Add a student" button 
26. Click on "Add a new person" button 
27. Enter the student's first name, last name and Gender. 
28. Click on the "Save" button and save the person details. 
29. Click on the "Save" button to save the enrollment details. 
30. Click on the student name. 
31. Click on the "View profile" button. 
32. Click on the "Create account" button. 
33. Enter the username. 
34. Enter the password. 
35. Click on the "Save button"
36. Clear the app storage. 
37. Login to the app using the student username and password. 
38. Verify the student able to see the content
39. Open the content
40. Complete the content
41. Clear the app storage. 
42. Login as admin.
43. On the library open the content
44. Click on "Attempts" tab 
45. Verify student content progress bar visible on attempts page 
46. Click on student name 
47. Verify the user able to see student attempts -duration,percent,score etc.
