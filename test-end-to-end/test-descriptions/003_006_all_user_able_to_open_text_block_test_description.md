# Text blocks can open and all text is visible

## Description:

All users on course should be able to open text blocks to see all text.

## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Launch Ustad app  
2. Click on "Existing User" button  
3. Click on "Enter link manually" button  
4. Enter the site link  
5. Login as admin.
6. Click on "Courses."
7. Click on the "+ Course" button.
8. Click on the "Add a new course" button.
9. Enter a course name.
10. Click on the "Description" field.
11. Enter the description.
12. Click on the "Done" button.
13. Click the "Save" button to save the course.
14. Click on the "Courses"
15. Click on the course name.
16. Click on the "Members" tab.
17. Click on the "Add a teacher" button
18. Click on "Add a new person" button
19. Enter the teacher's first name, last name and Gender.
20. Click on the "Save" button and save the person details.
21. Click on the "Save" button to save the enrollment details.
22. Click on the teacher name.
23. Click on the "View profile" button.
24. Click on the "Create account" button.
25. Enter the username.
26. Enter the password. 
27. Click on the "Courses"
28. Click on the course name. 
29. Click on the "Members" tab. 
30. Click on the "Add a student" button 
31. Click on "Add a new person" button 
32. Enter the student's first name, last name and Gender. 
33. Click on the "Save" button and save the person details. 
34. Click on the "Save" button to save the enrollment details. 
35. Click on the student name. 
36. Click on the "View profile" button. 
37. Click on the "Create account" button. 
38. Enter the username. 
39. Enter the password. 
40. Click on "Courses"
41. Click on Course name. 
42. Click on the "Edit" button. 
43. Click on the "Add block" button. 
44. Click on the "Module" button. 
45. Enter the title for Module block. 
46. Enter description. 
47. Click on "Done" to save the description. 
48. Click on "Done" to save the module block. 
49. Click on the "Add block" button. 
50. Click on the "Text" button. 
51. Enter the title for Text block. 
52. Click on "Description" field. 
53. Enter description. 
54. Click on "Done" to save the description. 
55. Enter text inside text editor field 
56. Click on "Done" to save the text block. 
57. Verify the module block and Text blocks are visible. 
58. Click on "More options" icon next to the text block. 
59. Click on "Indent" button. 
60. Click on "Save" to save the course.
61. Click on the text block
62. verify texts inside the test block is visible to admin user 
63. Clear app storage 
64. Login to the app using the student username and password.
65. Click on "Courses"
66. Click on course name. 
67. Click on the text block 
68. verify texts inside the test block is visible to student user 
69. Clear app storage 
70. Login to the app using the teacher username and password.
71. Click on "Courses"
72. Click on course name. 
73. Click on the text block 
74. verify texts inside the test block is visible to teacher user
