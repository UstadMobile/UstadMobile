# Assignment is set to allow multiple submissions

## Description:

If assignment is set to allow multiple submissions, student can make another submission after the first submission. Teacher can see the revised submission and give a new grade. The student can see the updated grade.
  
  ## Step-by-Step Procedure:

1. Launch Ustad app 
2. Click on "Existing User" button  
3. Click on "Enter link manually" button  
4. Enter the site link  
5. Login as admin.
6. Click on "Courses."
7. Click on the "+ Course" button.
8. Click on the "Add a new course" button.
9. Enter a course name.
10. Click on the "Description" field.
11. New text block appears.
12. Enter the description.
13. Click on the "Done" button.
14. Click "Save" button to save the course.
15. Click on the "Courses"
16. Click on the course name.
17. Click on the "Members" tab.
18. Click on the "Add a teacher" button
19. Click on "Add a new person" button
20. Enter the teacher's first name, last name and Gender.
21. Click on the "Save" button and save the person details.
22. Click on the "Save" button to save the enrollment details.
23. Click on the teacher name.
24. Click on the "View profile" button.
25. Click on the "Create account" button.
26. Enter the username.
27. Enter the password.
28. Click on the "Save button"
29. Click on "People".
30. Click on "+ Person" button.
31. Click on "Bulk import" button.
32. Click on "Select file" button.
33. Select the csv file.
34. Click on "Import"
35. Verify the import success message.
36. Clear the app storage
37. Login to the app using the teacher username and password.
38. Click on "Courses" tab.
39. Click on the course name.
40. Click on "Edit" button.
41. Click on "Add block" button
42. Click on "Assignment".
43. Enter the assignment title in the "Title" field.
44. Enter the assignment description in the "Description" field.
45. Set the submission policy to "Can make multiple submissions".
46. Click on the "Done" button.
47. Click on the "Save" button.
48. Clear the app storage
49. Login to the app using the student username and password.
50. Click on "Courses" tab.
51. Click on the course name.
52. Click on the assignment added by teacher.
53. Enter the assignment text in the provided text field.
54. Click on "Add file" button.
55. Attach the required file.
56. Click on the "Submit" button.
57. Verify the submit status changed to "Submitted".
58. Clear the app storage
59. Login to the app using the teacher username and password.
60. Click on "Courses" tab.
61. Click on the course name.
62. Click on the assignment.
63. Click on the "Submissions" tab.
64. Click on the specific student's name..
65. Review the submitted text
66. Open the submitted file.
67. Click on "Mark" field.
68. Enter the mark
69. Click on the "Submit grade" button.
70. Clear the app storage.
71. Login to the app using the student username and password.
72. Click on "Courses" tab.
73. Click on the course name.
74. Click on the assignment.
75. The student should see their graded assignment and the marks. 
76. Click on "Text" field 
77. Enter the assignment text. 
78. CLick on "Done" button. 
79. Click on "Submit" button. 
80. Verify the 2 submissions are visible to the user.
81. Clear the app storage 
82. Login to the app using the teacher username and password.
83. Click on "Courses" tab. 
84. Click on the course name. 
85. Click on the assignment. 
86. Click on the "Submissions" tab. 
87. Click on the specific student's name.. 
88. Review the submitted text 
89. Click on "Mark" field. 
90. Enter the new mark 
91. Click on the "Update grade" button. 
92. Clear the app storage. 
93. Login to the app using the student username and password.
94. Click on "Courses" tab. 
95. Click on the course name. 
96. Click on the assignment. 
97. Verify the student able see their updated mark.
